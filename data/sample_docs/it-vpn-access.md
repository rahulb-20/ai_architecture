# VPN Access and Password Reset

## Resetting your VPN password
Open the self-service portal at id.acme.example, choose "VPN credentials" and select Reset.
The new VPN password is active within 5 minutes. VPN passwords expire every 90 days.

## Multi-factor authentication
VPN login requires the Acme Authenticator app. Lost devices must be reported to the IT service desk on extension 4357.
