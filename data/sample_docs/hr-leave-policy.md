# Leave Policy (India)

## Annual leave
Full-time employees accrue 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited at 2 days per month.
Up to 10 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year. Days above 10 lapse on 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.
A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental leave
Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Non-birthing parents receive 6 weeks of paid leave.
